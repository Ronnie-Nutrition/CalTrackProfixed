# Privacy Policy for CalTrackPro

**Last Updated: October 27, 2024**

## Overview
CalTrackPro ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our nutrition tracking application.

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information
- **Profile Data**: Name, age, weight, height, and dietary goals that you voluntarily provide
- **Nutrition Data**: Food entries, recipes, and meal information you log

### Automatically Collected Information
- **Device Information**: Device type, operating system version
- **Usage Data**: App features used, frequency of use
- **Camera Access**: Only when you grant permission for barcode scanning or food photos

## How We Use Your Information
- To track your nutritional intake and progress
- To provide personalized dietary insights
- To save your recipes and meal history
- To improve app functionality and user experience

## Data Storage
- All personal data is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework
- If you enable iCloud sync, data is backed up to your personal iCloud account
- We do not have access to your personal data

## Third-Party Services
We use the Edamam API for nutrition information:
- Only food names are sent to Edamam for nutritional data lookup
- No personal information is shared with Edamam
- View Edamam's privacy policy at: https://www.edamam.com/privacy

## Data Security
- All data is encrypted on your device
- Network requests use HTTPS encryption
- We do not store data on our servers

## Your Rights
You can:
- Delete all app data through iOS Settings
- Disable camera permissions at any time
- Export your data (feature coming soon)
- Contact us with privacy concerns

## Children's Privacy
CalTrackPro is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy periodically. We'll notify you of significant changes through the app.

## Contact Us
For privacy concerns, contact: support@easyaiflows.com

## Compliance
This app complies with:
- Apple's App Store Guidelines
- iOS privacy requirements
- General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) principles
