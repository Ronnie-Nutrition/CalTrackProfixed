## App Description (4000 characters max)

CalTrackPro is your intelligent nutrition companion that makes calorie tracking effortless. Simply snap a photo of your food and let AI identify it instantly, or scan barcodes for accurate nutrition data from millions of products.

**SMART FOOD RECOGNITION**
• Take a photo of any meal and get instant nutrition information
• AI-powered recognition identifies foods automatically
• Works with home-cooked meals, restaurant dishes, and packaged foods
• Portion size estimation for accurate tracking

**BARCODE SCANNER**
• Scan product barcodes for instant nutrition lookup
• Access a database of millions of food products
• Quick and accurate – no manual entry needed

**COMPLETE NUTRITION TRACKING**
• Track calories, protein, carbs, and fat
• Set personalized daily goals based on your targets
• View progress with beautiful charts and insights
• Log breakfast, lunch, dinner, and snacks separately

**APPLE HEALTH INTEGRATION**
• Sync nutrition data with Apple Health
• Import weight and activity data
• See your complete health picture in one place

**RECIPE BUILDER**
• Create and save custom recipes
• Calculate nutrition per serving automatically
• Quick-add your favorite meals

**INSIGHTS & ANALYTICS**
• Weekly and monthly nutrition trends
• Identify patterns in your eating habits
• Celebrate streaks and milestones

**SYNC ACROSS DEVICES**
• iCloud sync keeps your data everywhere
• Start logging on iPhone, review on iPad
• Your data stays private and secure

Whether you're tracking macros for fitness goals, managing your weight, or simply eating healthier, CalTrackPro makes it simple. No complicated setup, no subscription required for core features – just smart, effortless nutrition tracking.

Download CalTrackPro today and take control of your nutrition!

## Review Notes for Apple

Demo Account: Not required – app works without login

Notes for Reviewer:
CalTrackPro is a nutrition tracking app that helps users log meals and monitor daily calorie intake. The app uses the device camera for two purposes:
1. Food recognition using on-device Vision/CoreML
2. Barcode scanning for product lookup

The app integrates with Apple Health to read/write nutrition data with user permission. All data is stored locally and in the user's private iCloud account.

No in-app purchases in version 1.0. Core features are free.
