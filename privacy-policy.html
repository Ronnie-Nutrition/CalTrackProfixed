CalTrackPro Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2025

CalTrackPro ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our iOS application.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Account Information: Email address and display name when you create an account using Sign in with Apple
  • Food & Nutrition Data: Food entries, meal logs, custom recipes, and nutrition information you input
  • Photos: Food images captured for AI-powered nutrition analysis (processed on-device when possible)
  • Profile Information: Weight, height, age, activity level, and nutrition goals you choose to provide

Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Information: Device type, iOS version, and app version for troubleshooting
  • Usage Data: Features used and general app interaction patterns (anonymized)

Health & Fitness Data (with your permission)

  • Apple Health data including weight, active calories, step count
  • Nutrition data synced to/from Apple Health
Important: Health data is stored locally on your device and in your personal iCloud account. We do not have access to your Apple Health data on our servers.

How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and personalize the calorie tracking experience
  • Analyze food photos to identify nutrition information
  • Calculate daily nutrition totals and progress toward goals
  • Sync your data across your Apple devices via iCloud
  • Generate insights and recommendations based on your nutrition patterns
  • Improve app performance and fix bugs

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored:

  • Locally on your device using iOS secure storage
  • In your personal iCloud account for cross-device sync (using Apple's CloudKit)
  • In Apple Health for nutrition data you choose to sync

We use industry-standard encryption and security measures. Your data is protected by Apple's security infrastructure.

Third-Party Services

CalTrackPro uses the following third-party services:

  • USDA FoodData Central: For nutrition database lookups (no personal data shared)
  • Open Food Facts: For barcode product lookups (no personal data shared)
  • Apple CloudKit: For secure data sync across your devices
  • Apple Health: For health data integration (with your explicit permission)

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

We may share data only:

  • When required by law or legal process
  • To protect our rights or safety
  • With service providers who assist app operations (under strict confidentiality)

Your Rights & Choices

  • Access: View all your data within the app
  • Deletion: Delete your account and all associated data from Settings
  • Export: Export your nutrition data from the app
  • Health Permissions: Manage Apple Health access in iOS Settings
  • Camera Access: Disable camera access in iOS Settings if you prefer manual entry

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account:

  • Local data is immediately removed from your device
  • iCloud data is deleted within 30 days
  • Apple Health data remains (managed through Apple Health app)

Children's Privacy

CalTrackPro is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of significant changes through the app or via email. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
